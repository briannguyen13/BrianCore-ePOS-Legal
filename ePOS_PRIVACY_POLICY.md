# Privacy Policy for BrianCore ePOS

Last updated: July 2026

BrianCore ePOS is a point-of-sale (POS) application designed for retail stores, restaurants, cafes and other businesses.

## Information We Collect

BrianCore ePOS may store business-related information entered by the user, including:

- Customer names
- Customer phone numbers
- Product information
- Sales transactions
- Inventory records

The application does not collect:

- Customer email addresses
- Social media account information
- Google account information
- Facebook account information

## Data Storage

All business data is stored locally on the user's device.

BrianCore ePOS does not transmit business data to any cloud service operated by BrianCore.

## Local Network Communication

BrianCore ePOS may synchronize data between devices on the same local area network (LAN) when this feature is enabled by the user.

This synchronization occurs only within the local network controlled by the business.

## Advertising

BrianCore ePOS does not display third-party advertisements.

## Security

We take reasonable measures to protect locally stored business information.
