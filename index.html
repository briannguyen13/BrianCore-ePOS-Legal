Privacy Policy for BrianCore ePOS

Last updated: July 2026

BrianCore ePOS is a point-of-sale (POS) application designed for retail stores, restaurants, cafes and other businesses.

Information We Collect

BrianCore ePOS may store business-related information entered by the user, including:

  • Customer names
  • Customer phone numbers
  • Product information
  • Sales transactions
  • Inventory records

The application does not collect:

  • Customer email addresses
  • Google account information
  • Facebook account information
  • Social media information

Data Storage

Business data is stored locally on the user's device. BrianCore ePOS does not transmit business data to any cloud service operated by BrianCore.

Local Network Communication

BrianCore ePOS may synchronize data between devices on the same local area network (LAN) when enabled by the user.

Advertising

BrianCore ePOS does not display third-party advertisements.

Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect locally stored business information.
